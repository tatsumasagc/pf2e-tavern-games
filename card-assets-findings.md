# Supplied Poppy’s Prize deck assets

The supplied ZIP contains 55 PNG files at 1536 × 2304 pixels: 52 standard suit-and-rank cards, two Pirate cards, and one card back.

The filenames map directly to the game’s four suits: `trees`, `ships`, `gems`, and `parrots`. Each suit contains `01` through `10`, `jack`, `queen`, and `king`; `01` visibly presents as an Ace and should therefore map to the engine rank `A` (value 14). `pirate_1.png` and `pirate_2.png` map to the two Pirate cards. `card_back.png` is a visually suitable card back for concealed hands and unrevealed common cards.

The card faces use a parchment-and-gold nautical visual style, while the card back has a multicoloured geometric field and central compass. The card display can use asset paths without modifying the supplied images.
