# Source Rules Map: Golem, Bounder, and Century

Source: [Archives of Nethys — Casino Games](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=1452), *Pathfinder #159: All or Nothing*, p. 76. Retrieved 28 August 2026.

## Golem

Golem uses a 52-card deck ranked 1–13 in four suits. It supports three to six players and a dealer. At the start of the night, the amulet goes to the player right of the dealer. Each player antes. The dealer deals five cards to each player. Starting at the amulet, players bet, match, raise, or fold until all have matched or folded. Remaining players discard up to two cards and receive replacements; discards form a face-down central pile. A second betting round starts at the amulet. If one player remains, that player wins the pot less the house’s 5% share. Otherwise, the remaining players reveal their hands and the dealer reveals the “golem hand,” which is the best five-card poker hand built from the discards. The player with the best hand must beat the golem hand to win. If they do not, that player adds twice the ante to the pot, cards are collected, and another hand is dealt only to the participants still in the game. When the pot is finally won, the house retains 5%, the amulet moves counterclockwise, and a new game begins.

## Bounder

Bounder uses three d6 for the dealer and two d20 for the shooter. The shooter puts forward a stake and rolls their first d20 to establish a point. The shooter may double their stake. Other participants may bet on the shooter’s point. The dealer rolls 3d6. If the dealer total equals the shooter’s point, the shooter and point bettors lose. A bet that the dealer rolls all odd or all even numbers pays 1× its stake; a three-of-a-kind dealer result pays 3× its stake. The shooter rolls their second d20. The shooter and point bettors win an amount equal to their stake if the two d20 results bracket the dealer result—one higher and one lower. A matching second d20 is a loss. A 1 and 20 pair doubles only the shooter’s stake gain. The next clockwise participant becomes shooter for the next game.

## User-Specified Drinking Contest

Each round, every participant presses Ready. When all are ready, each participant makes a blind-to-GM Performance check and a blind-to-GM Fortitude save. The Fortitude save is compared with a GM-selected DC. Critical Success reduces Stage by 1; Success has no stage change; Failure increases Stage by 1; Critical Failure increases Stage by 2. Stage six eliminates the participant.

Requested condition stages are: Stage 1, +1 item bonus to saves against fear for 10 minutes; Stage 2, off-guard plus that bonus for 10 minutes; Stage 3, clumsy 1, off-guard, and stupefied 2 for 10 minutes; Stage 4, clumsy 2 and sickened 2 for 10 minutes; Stage 5, clumsy 2, sickened 2, and stupefied 2 for 10 minutes; Stage 6, unconscious for 8 hours.

Drinking-contest cheating: any participant may elect to cheat. Their Fortitude result is automatically successful. Their blind Performance check is compared independently with the other participants’ Perception DCs. Each observer who detects the cheat and the GM are whispered separately; no message is sent for a successful comparison.
